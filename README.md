# Bankability Assessment — Kano Agro-Industrial Solar-plus-Storage Project (Sample)

A bankability assessment of a 5.0 MWp solar PV plant with 4.0 MWh battery storage,
supplying a commercial and industrial offtaker in Kano State under a 15-year US
dollar power purchase agreement. Prepared by CircularEnergies as a sample
deliverable.

## The transaction

- **Technology:** 5.0 MWp ground-mounted solar PV + 4.0 MWh battery storage
- **Offtake:** 15-year commercial and industrial PPA at USD 145/MWh, 2% annual escalation
- **Location:** Kano State, Nigeria
- **Total funding:** USD 6.09 million (66% senior debt against base capital cost, 60% against total funding)
- **Amortisation:** 14 years, sculpted to a constant pre-tax coverage ratio

- **`CE_Sample_Bankability_Report.pdf`** — The published assessment. Executive

## What this demonstrates

- Senior debt sizing sculpted to pre-tax cash flow, tested against a full downside envelope
- Nigerian pioneer relief and capital allowance treatment done correctly — allowances deferred through the relief period rather than exhausted, extending the tax-free window from three years to five
- Sensitivity analysis run against a debt service profile fixed at financial close, which is the correct basis for covenant testing
- Risk assessment structured for a credit committee, with the offtaker credit risk foregrounded as the dominant consideration
- Recommended conditions precedent aligned to the assessment findings, not a generic checklist

## The central finding

The capital structure protects the lender before it protects the sponsor. Debt
coverage holds above covenant in every downside case tested — including the
combined stress — while equity returns fall below hurdle in two of the four
downsides. Tariff is the binding commercial variable: a 10% tariff reduction
costs five points of equity IRR, against 2.4 points for a 7% generation shortfall
and 1.7 points for a 20% operating cost overrun.

Sponsors reviewing structures like this should recognise that coverage headroom
is not equity headroom.
