Central finding

The capital structure protects the lender before it protects the sponsor. Debt coverage holds above covenant in every downside tested - including the combined stress - while equity returns fall below hurdle in two of the four downsides. Tariff is the binding commercial variable: a 10% tariff reduction costs five points of equity IRR, against 2.4 points for a 7% generation shortfall and 1.7 points for a 20% operating cost overrun.

Coverage headroom is not equity headroom.
